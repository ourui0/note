"⼯程"的定义（ECPD / ABET）
The creative application of scientiﬁc principles to design or develop structures, machines, apparatus, or
manufacturing processes, or works utilizing them singly or in combination; or to construct or operate the same
with full cognizance of their design; or to forecast their behavior under speciﬁc operating conditions; all as
respects an intended function, economics of operation or safety to life and property.
