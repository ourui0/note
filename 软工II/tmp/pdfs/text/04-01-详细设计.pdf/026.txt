Rule of Thumb（设计抉择经验法则）
W hen there are alternative design choices, take a closer look at the cohesion and coupling implications of the
alternatives and possibly the future evolution pressures on the alternatives. Choose an alternative with good
cohesion, coupling and stability.
不存在"唯⼀正确"的设计 —— 只存在多个候选之间的权衡
耦合、内聚、稳定性是三⼤评价标尺
未来演化压⼒：考虑"哪个⽅案更经得起变更"
GRASP 给出的不是公式，⽽是讨论候选⽅案的框架
