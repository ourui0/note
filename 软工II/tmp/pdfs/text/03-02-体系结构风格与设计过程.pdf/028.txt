管道-过滤器⻛格（Pipe-and-Filter）[SW EBO K 2.2]
案例：Java Stream  API
List<String> titles = books.stream()
    .filter(b -> b.getPrice() > 50)
    .sorted(comparing(Book::getPrice))
    .map(Book::getTitle)
    .collect(toList());
