设计思维（Design Thinking）
Design is a form of problem-solving. Software design particularly faces W icked Problems — problems with no
deﬁnitive solution, only better or worse trade-oﬀs.
五步设计过程 （Ross, Goodenough & Irvine）
步骤 核⼼动作 软件设计产物
1 · Crystallize 明确⽬的与⽬标 功能需求 + 质量属性（NFR）
2 · Formulate 构想实现概念 体系结构⻛格 / 设计模式选择
3 · Devise 设计实现机制 类图 + 顺序图 + 算法选择
4 · Introduce 引⼊表达符号 UM L / 伪代码 / 接⼝定义语⾔（IDL ）
5 · Describe 描述具体使⽤场景设计⽂档 + 设计理由（Rationale）
这五步在体系结构设计和详细设计两个阶段都会反复出现 —— 详细设计就是把体系结构决策递归精化到可直接编码的粒
度。

Responsibilities（职责）
A responsibility is an obligation to perform a task（operational responsibility）or to maintain some data（data
responsibility）.
O perational responsibilities are usually fulﬁlled by operations（⽅法）.
Data responsibilities are usually fulﬁlled by attributes（属性）.
Class collaborations may be involved.
Responsibility-Driven Decomposition（职责驱动分解）
Responsibilities may be stated at diﬀerent levels of abstraction.
High-level responsibilities → high-level components；可逐层分解。
职责驱动的分解可能不同于功能分解 —— 职责同时反映"操作"与"数据"两⽅⾯的义务。

Responsibility Heuristics & Delegation
职责分配启发式
分配良好的职责 → ⾼内聚、低耦合
模块之间的职责不重叠
把操作和数据放在某个模块⾥，仅当它们帮助该模块履⾏
其职责
Delegation（委托）
Delegation is a tactic wherein one module (the
delegator) entrusts another module (the delegate)
with a responsibility.
⼀个对象可以保留⾼层职责，把具体执⾏委托给更专⻔的
对象
委托 = 解耦 + 关注点分离

协作（Collaboration）：本质
An application can be broken down into a set of many diﬀerent behaviors.
每⼀个⾏为由⼀组对象之间的协作实现
协作是对象⽹络中沿关系流动的消息模式，为完成某个⾏为⽽存在
协作分布在对象⽹络中，不存在于任何单⼀位置
Grady Booch（1992）："Equally important [as inheritance] is the invention of societies of objects that
responsibly collaborate with one another. These societies form what I call the mechanisms of a system, and
thus represent strategic architectural decisions because they transcend individual classes."

类型 关系 短语 解释 多重性 U ML
对象级 普通关联A has a B A ⻓期持有 B 引⽤ A: 0..* B: 0..* ──

GRASP Patterns
GRASP = General Responsibility Assignment Software Patterns
Not 'design patterns', rather fundamental principles of object design
Focus on one of the most important aspects of object design: assigning responsibilities to classes
5 个核⼼模式（本讲覆盖）
# 模式 解决的问题
1 L ow Coupling 降低模块之间的耦合
2 High Cohesion 保持模块职责的聚焦
3 Information Expert 把职责分配给"拥有信息"的类
4 Creator 由谁负责创建对象
5 Controller 由谁负责处理系统事件
Larman 著《UM L 和模式应⽤》是 O O  详设的经典参考。
📖  SW EBO K v4 §5.4 · O bject-O riented Design  |  §1.4 · Software Design Principles (Coupling, Cohesion)

--- PAGE 26 ---
Rule of Thumb（设计抉择经验法则）
W hen there are alternative design choices, take a closer look at the cohesion and coupling implications of the
alternatives and possibly the future evolution pressures on the alternatives. Choose an alternative with good
cohesion, coupling and stability.
不存在"唯⼀正确"的设计 —— 只存在多个候选之间的权衡
耦合、内聚、稳定性是三⼤评价标尺
未来演化压⼒：考虑"哪个⽅案更经得起变更"
GRASP 给出的不是公式，⽽是讨论候选⽅案的框架

--- PAGE 27 ---
① L ow Coupling & ② High Cohesion
L ow Coupling（低耦合）
Problem：如何减少变更的影响？如何⽀持低依赖、提⾼
复⽤？
Solution：分配职责时保持低耦合
若 B 已耦合于 A，则把新职责也给 A，不引⼊额外
依赖
优先依赖接⼝⽽⾮具体类（→ P4）
Eﬀect：某类的变更对其他类影响极⼩；类更易独⽴理解
与复⽤。
High Cohesion（⾼内聚）
Problem：如何让对象保持聚焦、易理解、易管理？
Solution：分配职责时保持⾼内聚
⼀个类只承担紧密相关的⼀组职责
不要为了复⽤⽽拼凑不相关的操作
Eﬀect：类的职责清晰，维护时改动范围⼩，⾃然导向低
耦合。
Low Coupling 与 High Cohesion 是 GRASP 的两个元原则 —— Information Expert、Creator、Controller 的取舍，最
终都以"是否提升内聚 / 降低耦合"为评判标准。
📖  SW EBO K v4 §1.4 · Coupling  |  §1.4 · Cohesion  |  §5.4 · O bject-O riented Design

③ Information Expert（信息专家）
Problem：What is the most basic principle by which responsibilities are assigned in object-oriented design?
Solution：Assign a responsibility to the class that has the information necessary to fulﬁll the responsibility.
案例
要计算 Sales.total() ：
谁知道每个 SalesLineItem 的数量？→ SalesLineItem
谁知道每个 Commodity 的价格？→ Commodity
→ Sales.total() 委托给 SalesLineItem.subtotal() ，后者再调 Commodity.getPrice()
"谁有数据，谁负责" —— ⾃然形成职责链，避免数据外泄。

④ Creator（创建者）
Q: W ho has responsibility to create an object?
A: By Creator, assign class B responsibility of creating instance of class A if（满⾜越多，优先级越⾼）：
B aggregates A objects
B contains A objects
B records instances of A objects
B closely uses A objects
B has the initializing data for creating A objects
where there is a choice, prefer B aggregates or contains A objects.
案例：Who is responsible for creating SalesLineItem objects? → Sale （because Sale contains
SalesLineItem，组合关系）。

⑤ Controller（控制器）
Problem：How to assign responsibility for handling a system event?
Solution：If a program receives events from external sources other than its graphical interface, add an event
class to decouple the event source(s) from the objects that actually handle the events.
4 种可选的控制器
1. The business or overall organization（façade controller）— e.g. Store
2. The overall "system"（façade controller）— e.g. POST
3. An animate thing in the domain that would perform the work（role controller）— e.g. Cashier
4. An artiﬁcial class (Pure Fabrication) representing the use case（use case controller）— e.g. BuyItemsHandler

Dispersed Control（分散式控制）
Characterized by having many components holding little data and having few responsibilities.
It is hard to understand the ﬂow of control.
Components are unable to do much on their own, increasing coupling.
It is hard to hide information.
Cohesion is usually poor.
Few modularity principles can be satisﬁed.
Heuristic（启发式经验）：Avoid interactions that require each component to send many messages —— 分散式控制
⼏乎在所有情况下都应该避免。

The program is gradually developed in a sequence of reﬁnement steps

Parnas · 层次结构与封装
Hierarchical Structure
模块或程序之间可定义某种关系
该关系是偏序

P1–P4 · 信息隐藏基础原则
编号 原则 含义
P1 全局变量有害 全局变量穿透所有模块，是隐藏的反义词
P2 优先显示表达 ⽤清晰的接⼝、命名、⽂档显式表达约束，⽽不是依赖隐式约定
