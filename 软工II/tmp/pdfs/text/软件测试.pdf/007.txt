Patriot missile defense 1991
• The Patriot missile defense system failed to defend 
against several missiles
• A small timing error in the system’s clock 
accumulated so that after 14 hours, the tracking 
system was no longer accurate
• In the attack on Dhahran, KSA, the system had 
been in operation for over 100 hours
• The system was mobile, so the developers did not 
expect it to remain in one place for 14 hours
